Personal data

This site sets no cookies, does not measure its audience and loads nothing from elsewhere: its typeface, images and script are served with it. It has no form.

Its host, GitHub, logs every visitor's IP address for security purposes, as for any GitHub Pages site (see GitHub's privacy statement). OffLok's files are downloaded from GitHub too.

The “Donate” button leads to Liberapay, a non-profit donation platform based in France: it receives the payment and handles the data that comes with it, under its own privacy policy. Liberapay does not tell LokLakh who its donors are, except for a payment by PayPal, which shows the recipient the payer's name and email address.
