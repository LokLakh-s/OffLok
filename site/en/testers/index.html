Test OffLok on Google Play

Before a new app can come out on Google Play, Google asks for a closed test: at least 12 people must try it for 14 days in a row. If you have an Android phone, you can help OffLok get there, in a few minutes.

You need a phone with Android 9 or later, and the Google account of its Play Store.

How to

  1. Join the testers' group, with your phone's Google account. This group is the list of testers for Google Play. Join the group
  2. Accept the test, with the same account. Become a tester If the page says the app is not available, the test is not open yet, or Google has not seen you in the group yet: try again a little later.
  3. Install OffLok from Google Play, then turn it on. Android asks you to accept its VPN connection: OffLok uses it to answer the phone's DNS, and nothing else goes through it. OffLok on Google Play
  4. Keep it for 14 days, using your phone as usual. Do not leave the test before: a tester who leaves early does not count.

A problem, an idea?

Write to tor@offlok.org, or open an issue on GitHub. A site that stopped working, an unclear message, a draining battery: it all helps.

And then?

Once the 14 days are over, Google reviews the test, then OffLok comes out on Google Play for everyone. You keep it: it updates through the Play Store, like your other apps.

Your privacy

Joining the group gives your Gmail address to Google and to LokLakh, which publishes OffLok: it is only used to open the test to you. You can leave the group whenever you like. The app itself collects nothing: see the privacy policy.
